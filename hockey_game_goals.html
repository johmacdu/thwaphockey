Game Plan

Today’s four.

These are the four things to notice, try, and compete on today. No grading. Just play, then talk about what happened.

Scan Twice

Shoulder check before retrieving the puck. Find pressure, teammates, and open ice. Check again before you touch it.

Have a picture before you get it.
🧠

Know Before You Get It

Look around before the pass arrives and have an idea of your next play. You can still change your mind.

See first. Touch second.
2️⃣

First + Second Option

Find your best play and a backup before you receive the puck.

Always have Plan B.
🕵️

Learn an Opponent

Study someone you keep facing. Notice a pattern, remember it, then use it later.

I noticed a pattern, then I used it.
🐢

Slow the Game Down

Find moments when everyone looks rushed but you actually have time. Use the space.

You don't have to play at everyone else's speed.
🧩

Find Another Solution

If the same play keeps failing, change the answer.

Solve the problem, don't repeat it.
🎓

Teach Dad Something

Notice something during the game Dad probably cannot see from the stands. Explain it after.

Be the hockey detective.
🪄

Move the Defender

Use your eyes, shoulders, stick, puck position, or speed to make a defender move, then attack the space he leaves.

Move him before you beat him.
🧲

Make Him Choose

If you have time, let the opponent commit first. Then use the space his decision creates.

Don't rush his decision.
🧲

Pull Someone With You

Carry toward a defender on purpose. When he commits to you, move the puck into the space you created.

Carry with a purpose.
🐢⚡

Change Speed

Slow down, speed up, hesitate, or change direction. Don't attack every defender at one speed.

Fast isn't the only weapon.
🛣️

Take the Ice

If nobody challenges you, take the open ice until somebody has to stop you.

Make them take it away.
↩️

Escape One

Beat the first forechecker with your feet, edges, cutback, the net, a pass, or deception instead of throwing it away.

Beat pressure. Keep possession.
🚫

No Automatic Boards

Before going up the wall, check middle, D partner, reverse, weak side, and carry.

Look before boards.
⚡

Move It Early

If the right pass is already there, make it before pressure arrives.

Quick can be smarter.
🛣️

Carry or Pass?

If there's open ice, take it. If a teammate is in a better spot, move it.

Read it. Choose it.
🔄

Defense → Offense

The instant you win possession, change your thinking from defend to attack.

Can we keep it and attack?
🚀

Pass + Go

After a good breakout pass, move your feet immediately. Try to beat an opponent up the ice and become another option.

Pass it. Then GO.

Fourth Attacker

When your team clearly controls the puck, find safe opportunities to join as the next attacking layer.

Join when it's green.
