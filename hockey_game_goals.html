Your Game Plan

These are the things to notice, try, and compete on today. No grading. Just play, then talk about what happened.

📣
Coach’s goal for the team

👀

Scan Twice

Shoulder check before retrieving the puck. Find pressure, teammates, and open ice. Check again before you touch it.

Have a picture before you get it.
🧠

Know Before You Get It

Look around before the pass arrives and have an idea of your next play. You can still change your mind.

See first. Touch second.
2️⃣

First + Second Option

Find your best play and a backup before you receive the puck.

Always have Plan B.
🕵️

Learn an Opponent

Study someone you keep facing. Notice a pattern, remember it, then use it later.

I noticed a pattern, then I used it.
🐢

Slow the Game Down

Find moments when everyone looks rushed but you actually have time. Use the space.

You don't have to play at everyone else's speed.
🧩

Find Another Solution

If the same play keeps failing, change the answer.

Solve the problem, don't repeat it.
🎓

Teach Dad and Mom

Notice something during the game Dad and Mom probably cannot see from the stands. Explain it to them after.

Be the hockey detective.
🤝

Make One Teammate Better

Pick one teammate before the game. Help that player succeed with good passes, support, communication, and space.

Help someone have a great game.
🗣️

Be Their Eyes

Give useful information before teammates receive the puck: TIME, MAN, MIDDLE, WHEEL, or your team's words.

Talk before they get it.
🎁

Create for Someone Else

Instead of only asking how you can score, look for ways to put teammates into dangerous positions.

Make the next player better.
🦸🚫

No Superhero Hockey

Don't try to fix every mistake by doing everyone's job. Play correctly, support teammates, and trust them.

Don't save the game alone.
😤🚫

Zero Frustration

Your mistake or a teammate's mistake: no head drop, stick slam, glare, or complaining. Get into the next play.

Body language leads.
🔥

Change the Momentum

If the team is struggling, create momentum without trying to score by yourself.

Create the next good play.
➡️

Win the Next Shift

Ignore the scoreboard. Every time you come over the boards, make the next shift good.

Next shift.
🗣️

Positive Voice

When things aren't going well, communicate confidence: We're good. Let's get one. Next shift.

Bring the team with you.
♻️

Response After Mistake

Mistakes happen. Measure the next five seconds, not the mistake.

Win the next five seconds.
🪄

Move the Defender

Use your eyes, shoulders, stick, puck position, or speed to make a defender move, then attack the space he leaves.

Move him before you beat him.
🧲

Make Him Choose

If you have time, let the opponent commit first. Then use the space his decision creates.

Don't rush his decision.
🧲

Pull Someone With You

Carry toward a defender on purpose. When he commits to you, move the puck into the space you created.

Carry with a purpose.
🐢⚡

Change Speed

Slow down, speed up, hesitate, or change direction. Don't attack every defender at one speed.

Fast isn't the only weapon.
🛣️

Take the Ice

If nobody challenges you, take the open ice until somebody has to stop you.

Make them take it away.
🤝

Pass + Move

After every pass, immediately find useful space and become an option again.

Pass. Move. Get it back.
🔁

Give-and-Go Hunter

Create at least three deliberate give-and-go opportunities.

Beat them with two players.
🎁

Create Someone Else's Chance

Create dangerous chances for teammates. They don't have to score for it to count.

A great chance counts.
👻

Find the Weak Side

Look away from the puck and find the player or space everyone else has forgotten.

See what others miss.
🏒

Drive the Net

Skate hard straight to the front of the net. Good things happen when you get there first.

Beat them to the net.
🧍

Set a Screen

Stand where the goalie can't see the puck. Make the shot a surprise.

Be the one he can't see around.
↩️

Hunt the Rebound

After a shot, go where the puck might bounce. Loose pucks near the net are chances.

Go where it bounces.
🥢

Tip It In

Get your stick on shots coming from your teammates. A little touch can change everything.

Get a stick on it.
↩️

Escape One

Beat the first forechecker with your feet, edges, cutback, the net, a pass, or deception instead of throwing it away.

Beat pressure. Keep possession.
🚫

No Automatic Boards

Before going up the wall, check middle, D partner, reverse, weak side, and carry.

Look before boards.
⚡

Move It Early

If the right pass is already there, make it before pressure arrives.

Quick can be smarter.
🛣️

Carry or Pass?

If there's open ice, take it. If a teammate is in a better spot, move it.

Read it. Choose it.
🔄

Defense → Offense

The instant you win possession, change your thinking from defend to attack.

Can we keep it and attack?
🚀

Pass + Go

After a good breakout pass, move your feet immediately. Try to beat an opponent up the ice and become another option.

Pass it. Then GO.
4️⃣

Fourth Attacker

When your team clearly controls the puck, find safe opportunities to join as the next attacking layer.

Join when it's green.
🚦

Green / Yellow / Red

Green: we clearly have it. Yellow: puck is uncertain. Red: they have it.

GO / READ / DEFEND
⏰

Arrive Late

Let defenders follow the first attackers and arrive into the space that opens behind them.

Timing creates space.
👻

Find the Weak Side

As the play moves to one side, look for open ice or a teammate away from everyone's attention.

Look away from the crowd.
🧱

Don't Back Up Forever

Stay close enough to challenge the attacker. Try to stop the rush before it gets deep into your zone.

Close the gap.
🏠

Protect the Middle

You don't always need to steal the puck. First take away the dangerous middle.

Middle first.
♻️

Win It Back

Turnover? No frustration. Immediately become the hardest player trying to recover possession.

Next five seconds.
🎯

Control, Don't Chase

Use position, skating, and your stick to control the attacker's choices instead of chasing the puck.

Take away what he wants.
