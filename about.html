Vancouver Jr. Rangers 10U

Train at home. Be a problem to play against.

Thwap is a young player's hockey world, with the whole team pulling the same way.

What a kid does

Three skills a day, plus their own goals.

🏒

Hands

Stickhandling reps picked for the day. Check them off and build a streak.

🥅

Shooting

Shooting reps to sharpen accuracy and a quicker release.

⚡

Dryland

Off-ice footwork and conditioning so they move better on the ice.

🎯

Game Day Goals

Before each game the kid picks a couple of their own things to work on, goals they can actually remember on the ice.

Live demo. Tap around, it really works.

How the team trains together

Everybody fills the same bar.

01 / 04

One shared total.

Every rep anybody on the team does adds to one team total. The more everyone trains, the higher the whole team climbs.

02 / 04

Pick each other up.

Send a cheer when a teammate puts in the work, or call them on fire when they are rolling. Support, never a ranking.

03 / 04

The whole team, one place.

Everybody on one roster, each player with their own card. You always know who is in it with you.

04 / 04

Streaks keep it rolling.

Train day after day and the streak climbs. Get hot enough and you are on fire, and the whole team feels it.

For coaches

Run it from your phone.

01 / 03

See who trained.

Your team at a glance: who put in the work this week and who needs a nudge.

02 / 03

Set the week.

Pick the drills and the cadence, and mark each skill required or optional. The plan runs itself for every player.

03 / 03

Give every player a focus.

Build each kid an IDP, one focus to work on, set from the 10U skill presets. The team plan is yours; the game-day goals are theirs.

Who made it

A kid on the Jr. Rangers 10U helped design it, build it, and test it. Made by someone who plays, for everyone who does.
