Vancouver Jr. Rangers 10U

Train at home. Be a problem to play against.

Thwap is a young player's hockey world, with the whole team pulling the same way.

What a kid does

Three things, about 30 minutes.

🏒

Train today

Stickhandling, shooting, and dryland, picked for today. Check them off and build your streak.

🎯

Set your Game Day Goals

Before each game you pick your own couple of things to work on, goals you can actually remember on the ice.

⭐

Build your card

Every bit of training fills your player card and earns stickers. Your hockey, your card.

Live demo. Tap around, it really works.

How the team wins together

The whole team pulls the same way.

Everybody fills the same bar.

Every drill anybody on the team does adds to one team total. The more everyone trains, the higher the whole team climbs together.

Teammates hype each other up.

Send a cheer when somebody puts in the work, or call a teammate on fire when they are rolling. Support, never a ranking.

For coaches

Run it from your phone.

See your whole team at a glance.

Who trained this week, who is leading, and who needs a nudge. The whole roster on one screen, straight from your phone.

Set the plan and each player's focus.

Pick the drills and the weekly cadence, set the team's game-day goal, and give each player an IDP, their one focus to work on. Built for a 10U team, not a spreadsheet.

Who made it

Built by a player. Not by crazy hockey parents.

A kid on the Jr. Rangers 10U helped design it, build it, and test it. Made by someone who plays, for everyone who does.

Compete. Improve. Have fun.
