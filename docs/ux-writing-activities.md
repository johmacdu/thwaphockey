# Thwap Hockey - UX Writing: All Activities

> The on-site copy for every drill and exercise. Written for players ages 8-13.
> Card pattern: Collapsed = Emoji + Title + Dose. Expanded = Intent (one-liner) + Numbered Steps (bold label + instruction).

# SHOOTING (20 Drills, 4-Day Rotation)

## Day 1: Shooting While Moving

### 1. Moving Warm-Up (dose: 6-8 shots, different angles)
Intent: Get comfortable shooting without stopping your feet.
1. Start: 15-20 feet from the net with a puck.
2. Move: Stickhandle toward the net. Keep your feet moving the whole time.
3. Pick a corner: Don't stop. Shoot with your feet still going.
4. Reset: Grab another puck and go again from a different angle.

### 2. Change the Angle (dose: 6 each side)
Intent: Move the puck around something and shoot right away from the new angle.
1. Set up: Put a cone between you and the net.
2. Come in: Carry the puck toward the cone with your feet moving.
3. Move it: Pull or push the puck around it to one side.
4. Shoot: As soon as the puck clears it, shoot.
5. Switch sides: Do it the other way.

### 3. Random Target (dose: 8-10 shots)
Intent: Start moving before you know where you're shooting.
1. Set up: Create four target areas on the net.
2. Move: Start with the puck. Keep your head up.
3. Listen: Have Thwap, a parent, or a partner call out a target.
4. Find it: Look at the target with your eyes.
5. Shoot: Don't stop. Reset and get a new target.

### 4. Under Two Seconds (dose: 8-10 reps, different spots)
Intent: Get the puck off your stick fast. No overthinking.
1. Ready: Start with your stick on the ice in a ready position.
2. Get: Have someone pass you the puck (or use a rebounder).
3. Control it: Catch it, find your target.
4. Fire: Release the shot in under two seconds.
5. Reset: Move to a different spot and do it again.

### 5. Ten Different Goals (dose: 10 goals)
Intent: Score 10 goals without using the same release twice in a row.
1. Start: Grab a puck. Move before you shoot.
2. Score: Pick a way to attack and score a goal.
3. Change it: On the next one, change something: angle, release point, forehand, backhand, pull, or push.
4. Keep going: Score 10 different goals. If you repeat yourself, it doesn't count!

## Day 2: Backhand

### 6. Backhand Basics (dose: 8-10 shots)
Intent: Learn to load the puck on your backhand and release it cleanly.
1. Set up: Stand 8-10 feet from the net. Put the puck on your backhand.
2. Cup it: Cup the puck with the blade. Hands slightly away from your body.
3. Weight: Shift your weight toward the net.
4. Sweep: Sweep through the puck.
5. Finish: End with the blade pointing at your target.

### 7. Backhand Accuracy (dose: work all 4 corners)
Intent: Use your backhand to hit different spots on the net on purpose.
1. Start close: Get near the net. Puck on your backhand.
2. Look first: Look at your target before you shoot.
3. Shoot: Aim for one corner.
4. Reset: Pick a different target.
5. All four: Work your way around all four corners.

### 8. Moving Backhand (dose: 6-8 shots, different angles)
Intent: Shoot a backhand while your feet are still going.
1. Carry it: Start 15 feet from the net. Move the puck toward it.
2. Keep moving: Don't stop your feet.
3. Backhand: Move the puck to your backhand. Hands away from your body.
4. Shoot: Shoot without stopping.
5. Repeat: Try coming in from different angles.

### 9. Forehand to Backhand (dose: 6 each side)
Intent: Show your forehand first, then switch to your backhand and shoot.
1. Come in: Come in with the puck on your forehand.
2. Pull across: Pull the puck across your body onto your backhand.
3. Don't wait: No extra stickhandle, shoot right away.
4. Mix it up: Try it from straight on and from an angle.

### 10. Backhand Only (dose: 10 backhand goals)
Intent: The only way to score is with a backhand. Nothing else counts.
1. Start anywhere: Puck on either side. Move toward the net.
2. Create space: Stickhandle however you want to find an opening.
3. Backhand: Move the puck to your backhand.
4. Finish: It only counts if the final shot is a backhand.
5. Goal: Try to score 10 backhand goals.

## Day 3: In Close

### 11. Quick Hands (dose: 6-8 shots, alternating sides)
Intent: Control the puck close to the net without needing lots of room.
1. Start close: A few feet from the net. Keep the puck near your body.
2. Quick moves: Two or three quick stickhandles.
3. Outside: Move the puck outside your feet.
4. Shoot: Shoot right away.
5. Switch: Reset on the other side and do it again.

### 12. Catch and Release (dose: 6-8 reps, different pass angles)
Intent: Get a pass near the net and release it before the goalie can reset.
1. Ready: Start near the net with your stick ready.
2. Get: Get a pass from the side. Catch it soft on your blade.
3. No extras: Don't add extra stickhandles.
4. Fire: Shoot right away.
5. Mix it up: Change where the passes come from each time.

### 13. Pull Across (dose: 6 each direction)
Intent: Pull the puck across your body before finishing from the new angle.
1. Come in: Start a few feet from the net with the puck on one side.
2. Sell it: Fake the shot from that side.
3. Pull: Pull the puck across your body. Keep your hands away from your feet.
4. Shoot: Shoot as soon as you create the new angle.
5. Switch: Switch directions each time.

### 14. Forehand / Backhand Finish (dose: 8-10 reps)
Intent: Go to the net and decide at the last second which side you'll use.
1. Move in: Start with the puck in front of you. Head toward the net.
2. Head up: Keep your eyes up.
3. Decide late: At the last moment, pick forehand or backhand.
4. Finish: Move the puck to that side and finish quick.
5. Mix it up: Don't always pick your favorite side.

### 15. Rebound Finish (dose: 6-8 reps)
Intent: Stay near the net after your first shot and finish the rebound.
1. Shoot low: Shoot low to create a rebound. Don't watch your shot.
2. Move in: Get toward the net right away.
3. Find it: Find the rebound. Get your stick on it.
4. Finish: Use whichever side is open.
5. Reset: Only reset after the second try.

## Day 4: Puck Skills + Shooting

### 16. Carry / Pass / Shoot (dose: 6-8 reps, different angles)
Intent: Connect carrying, passing, and shooting into one play.
1. Carry: Start away from the net. Move the puck forward with your feet going.
2. Pass: Pass to a parent, partner, wall, or rebounder.
3. Move: Move right after you pass. Get your stick ready for the return.
4. Get and shoot: Get the puck back and shoot quickly.

### 17. Bad Puck Challenge (dose: 8-10 reps)
Intent: Learn to shoot pucks that aren't sitting perfectly on your blade.
1. Messy pass: Have a parent or partner send you a messy puck. It can bounce, roll, go in your feet, or end up too far ahead.
2. Find it: Get your blade on the puck.
3. Quick control: Control it as fast as you can.
4. Shoot: Shoot without trying to make everything perfect.
5. Again: Get a different kind of bad puck each time.

### 18. Angle Attack (dose: 6 each side)
Intent: Carry the puck toward a cone, move around it, and shoot from the new lane.
1. Set up: Place a cone between you and the net.
2. Attack: Carry the puck toward it. Wait until you're close.
3. Escape: Move the puck quickly to one side. Move your body with it.
4. Shoot: Shoot from the new lane.
5. Switch: Switch sides each time.

### 19. Random Target (dose: 8-10 shots)
Intent: Let Thwap decide where your shot goes after the play has already started.
1. Go: Start with a puck. Move toward the net.
2. Target: Thwap randomly shows a target.
3. Find it: Look at the target with your eyes.
4. Shoot: Adjust the puck if you need to and shoot quickly.
5. Keep moving: Don't stop your feet just to hit the target.

### 20. Quick Release Challenge (dose: 5 good quick releases in a row)
Intent: See how fast you can turn a pass into a quality shot.
1. Start empty: No puck on your blade.
2. Get: Get a pass. Control it. Find the net.
3. Fire: Shoot in under two seconds.
4. Quality: It only counts if it's smooth and on target.
5. Streak: Try to string together 5 good quick releases in a row.

# STICKHANDLING (19 Drills)

## Fundamental Rhythm

### 1. Stationary Side-to-Side (dose: 30 seconds)
Intent: Move the puck side to side across your body. Quick and smooth.
1. Stance: Ready position, knees bent, hands out front.
2. Move it: Slide the puck side to side across your body.
3. Stay quick: Small, fast touches. Head up.

### 2. Front-to-Back (dose: 30 seconds)
Intent: Push the puck out in front, then pull it back to your toes.
1. Push out: Reach the puck straight out in front.
2. Pull back: Draw it back to your toes.
3. Keep rhythm: Smooth push-pull, soft hands.

### 3. The "L" Shape (dose: 30 seconds each side)
Intent: Push forward, drag back, then push wide to one side.
1. Forward: Push the puck out in front.
2. Back: Drag it back to you.
3. Wide: Push it out to one side to make an L.

### 4. Wide Extensions (dose: 30 seconds)
Intent: Reach as far to each side as you can, forehand and backhand.
1. Forehand: Reach the puck out wide on your strong side.
2. Backhand: Reach it out wide the other way.
3. Shift weight: Lean into each reach. Stay balanced.

### 5. Narrow-to-Wide (dose: 30 seconds)
Intent: Three tiny stickhandles, then push the puck out wide.
1. Narrow: 3 quick, tiny touches in tight.
2. Wide: Push the puck out wide right away.
3. Repeat: Tiny-tiny-tiny, then explode wide.

## Multi-Directional

### 6. Figure-8 Around Cones (dose: 1 minute)
Intent: Guide the puck in a figure-8 around two cones. Keep it flowing.
1. Set up: Two cones about a stick-length apart.
2. Weave: Guide the puck in a figure-8 around them.
3. Smooth: Keep it flowing. No stops.

### 7. The Triangle Drill (dose: 1 minute)
Intent: Move the puck in a big triangle around your feet.
1. Shape: Picture a big triangle around your feet.
2. Trace it: Move the puck from point to point.
3. Control: Go slow and clean before you try fast.

### 8. The Diamond Matrix (dose: 1 minute)
Intent: Four cones in a diamond, weave the puck around each point.
1. Set up: 4 cones in a diamond shape.
2. Weave: Take the puck around each point.
3. Head up: Glance up between points.

### 9. Z-Pattern Slalom (dose: length of floor, 3 reps)
Intent: Move the puck in a zig-zag, forward-left, then forward-right.
1. Zig: Push the puck forward-left, then forward-right.
2. Zig-zag: Keep the Z going down the floor.
3. Quick feet: Move with the puck.

### 10. The Square Box (dose: 1 minute)
Intent: Trace a square with the puck, front, side, back, side.
1. Front: Push the puck forward.
2. Side: Move it out to the side.
3. Back then side: Pull it back, then across. Trace the whole box.

## Strength & Control

### 11. Top-Hand Only (dose: 30 seconds)
Intent: Control the puck with only your top hand on the stick.
1. One hand: Top hand only on the stick.
2. Control: Small, smooth moves.
3. Build strength: Feel your forearm work.

### 12. Bottom-Hand Only (dose: 30 seconds)
Intent: Control the puck with only your bottom hand on the stick.
1. One hand: Bottom hand only.
2. Guide it: Steer the puck around.
3. Soft touch: Light grip, not stiff.

### 13. Toe Drag & Rescue (dose: 30 seconds)
Intent: Push the puck wide, then hook it back with the toe of your blade.
1. Push wide: Send the puck out to the side.
2. Toe hook: Use the toe of the blade to pull it back.
3. Rescue it: Reel it back under control.

### 14. Heel-to-Toe Flips (dose: 10 attempts)
Intent: Roll the puck from heel to toe and pop it up.
1. Heel: Start the puck at the heel of the blade.
2. Roll: Roll it out toward the toe.
3. Lift: Give it a small pop as it reaches the toe.

### 15. Flip Over Objects (dose: 10 attempts)
Intent: Chip the puck or ball over a stick or low barrier.
1. Barrier: Lay a stick flat on the floor.
2. Chip: Gently lift the puck over it.
3. Catch it: Settle it on the other side.

## Reaction & Game

### 16. Eyes-Up Glancing (dose: 1 minute)
Intent: Stickhandle without looking at the puck. Eyes on a target.
1. Eyes up: Look at a target, a parent, the wall, anything. Not the puck.
2. Feel it: Trust your hands to find the puck.
3. Hold it: Keep your head up the whole time.

### 17. The Wall Bounce (dose: 1 minute)
Intent: Pass a ball against a wall and catch the rebound soft.
1. Pass: Send the ball into the wall.
2. Catch it soft: Catch the rebound soft.
3. Repeat: Quick, smooth, and steady.

### 18. Cone Weave Course (dose: 3 full runs)
Intent: Line up 5 cones and weave the puck through them.
1. Set up: 5 cones in a straight line.
2. Weave: Take the puck through each one.
3. Speed up: Go faster once it feels clean.

### 19. Chaos Dribbling (dose: 1 minute)
Intent: Drop 10 random things on the floor and weave through them.
1. Scatter: Drop about 10 items on the floor.
2. Weave: Move through them any way you can.
3. React: No set path. Read and go.

# DRYLAND (16 Exercises)

### 1. Will's Squares (4-sq Drill) (dose: 20 seconds / pattern)
Intent: Quick, light hops through the square. Head up. 20 seconds each.
1. Set up: Mark a square on the floor. Number the corners 1, 2, 3, 4. Stay low.
2. Both feet: Hop 1-2, then 1-3, then 1-4. Do each for 20 seconds.
3. Right foot only: Same patterns. Hop 1-2, 1-3, 1-4. 20 seconds each.
4. Left foot only: Same thing. Hop 1-2, 1-3, 1-4. 20 seconds each.
5. Both feet, long patterns: 1-2-3, 1-4-2, 1-2-4-3 and more. 20 seconds each. Push hard, land light, head up.

### 2. Skater Jumps (dose: 8x, 2 reps)
Intent: Jump sideways, stick the landing.
1. Start: One leg, knee bent, like a skater push-off.
2. Jump: Jump sideways and land soft on the other leg.
3. Stick it: Bend the knee. Hold your balance for one second.
4. Back: Jump back the other way. Control matters more than distance.

### 3. Broad Jumps (dose: 8x, 2 reps)
Intent: Jump forward as far as you can. Land soft.
1. Stand tall: Feet about hip-width apart.
2. Load up: Swing your arms back and bend your knees.
3. Explode: Swing your arms up. Jump as far as you can.
4. Land soft: Both feet, knees bent. Hold the landing before the next jump.

### 4. Box Jumps (dose: 5x, 2 reps)
Intent: Jump up onto a low box. Land quiet.
1. Face it: Stand facing a sturdy low box or step.
2. Load: Bend your knees and swing your arms back.
3. Jump: Jump up and land soft on top with both feet, knees bent.
4. Step down: Stand up tall. Step (don't jump) back down. Reset and repeat.

### 5. Reverse Lunges (dose: 8x, 2 reps)
Intent: Step back, drop down, drive up.
1. Stand tall: Hands on hips or out for balance.
2. Step back: Step one foot straight back. Lower until both knees are an L shape.
3. Stay tall: Keep your chest up and front knee over your ankle, not past your toes.
4. Drive up: Push through the front heel to stand back up. Switch legs each rep.

### 6. Single-Leg RDL (dose: 5x, 2 reps, each side)
Intent: Balance on one leg. Tip forward like a seesaw.
1. Balance: Stand on one leg with a soft bend in that knee.
2. Tip: Bend at the hips. Reach toward the floor as your back leg lifts behind you.
3. Stay level: Keep your back flat and hips level, like a seesaw tipping.
4. Stand up: Squeeze the standing leg to stand back up tall. Do all reps, then switch legs.

### 7. Wall Sit (dose: 45 seconds)
Intent: Sit against the wall. No chair allowed.
1. Back flat: Put your back flat against a wall and walk your feet out.
2. Slide down: Slide down until your knees make an L shape, like sitting in a chair.
3. Hold: Keep your back flat on the wall. Weight in your heels.
4. Breathe: Hold for the time. Breathe steady. Then stand up and shake it out.

### 8. Sit-ups (dose: 30, 2 reps)
Intent: Curl up with your belly, not your neck.
1. Down: Lie on your back, knees bent, feet flat on the floor.
2. Arms: Cross your arms or reach your hands toward your knees.
3. Curl: Curl up slowly using your belly, not your neck.
4. Lower: Lower back down with control. Breathe out on the way up.

### 9. Plank with Shoulder Taps (dose: 30 seconds, 2 reps)
Intent: Hold a strong plank. Tap the opposite shoulder. Don't rock.
1. Set up: Get on your hands and toes, arms straight, body in one straight line.
2. Stay strong: Squeeze your belly so your hips don't sag or twist.
3. Tap across: Lift one hand and tap the opposite shoulder. Put it back down.
4. Switch hands: Tap the other way. Keep going, slow and steady. Try not to rock your hips.

### 10. Stair Sprints (dose: 10 yards, 6 reps)
Intent: Run up the stairs fast. Walk back down slow.
1. Start: Find a safe set of stairs and start at the bottom.
2. Sprint: Run up quick and light, one or two steps at a time. Pump your arms.
3. Eyes up: Keep your eyes up and steps smooth.
4. Recover: Walk back down slowly to recover. Then go again.

### 11. Sprint Starts (dose: 4 reps)
Intent: Explode into your first few steps as fast as you can.
1. Stance: Ready position, one foot slightly back, knees bent, leaning forward.
2. Go: On "go," drive your back leg and pump your arms hard.
3. Explode: Burst into a few fast, powerful steps.
4. Reset: Slow down safely, walk back, and reset before the next start.

### 12. Reaction Start (dose: ball drop, before the second bounce)
Intent: Your partner drops a ball. Catch it before the second bounce.
1. Face up: Face a partner a few steps away. They hold a ball out.
2. Ready: Stay in a ready position, eyes on the ball, ready to move.
3. Go: When they drop it, sprint and try to catch it before the second bounce.
4. Again: Reset and go again. React to the drop, don't guess.

### 13. Split Squat Hold (dose: 2 x 20 seconds)
Intent: Drop into a lunge and hold it. Don't come up.
1. Lunge: Step into a lunge, one foot forward, one back.
2. Drop: Lower straight down until both knees are an L shape.
3. Hold: Keep your chest tall and weight balanced between both legs.
4. Switch: Hold still for the time, then switch legs.

### 14. Calf Raises (dose: 12x, 2 reps)
Intent: Push up tall on your toes. Come down slow.
1. Stand tall: Feet about hip-width apart. Use a wall for balance.
2. Up: Push up onto the balls of your feet as high as you can.
3. Pause: Pause at the top for a second.
4. Down: Lower slowly back down with control. Repeat.

### 15. Dead Bugs (dose: 8 each side)
Intent: Opposite arm and opposite leg go down. Slow and smooth.
1. Start: Lie on your back, arms reaching up, knees bent up over your hips.
2. Press: Press your low back into the floor. Keep it there.
3. Lower: Slowly lower one arm overhead and the opposite leg toward the floor.
4. Switch: Bring them back and switch sides. Keep it slow.

### 16. McDavid's (dose: 60 seconds, 3 reps)
Intent: Feet moving. Hands working. Eyes up. Call out the numbers.
1. Stance: Get low in a hockey stance. Keep your feet moving fast. Shuffle or crossover over a line.
2. Stickhandle: Stickhandle a ball or puck right in front of you. Soft, fast touches.
3. Eyes up: Keep your eyes on your partner. Call out every number they flash.
4. All at once: Keep your chest facing forward. Let your hands and feet do their thing. Your eyes just read the numbers.
