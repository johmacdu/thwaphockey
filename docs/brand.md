# Brand — Thwap Hockey Training

## What this is
A home-practice app for the **Vancouver Jr. Rangers 10U** (Washington) youth
hockey team. Players are **9–12 years old**. Parents may help the youngest, but
the app talks to the **player**, not the parent. See `child-centered-design.md`
for the stance behind every choice.

## Name
- App / brand name: **Thwap Hockey Training** (renamed from "Bigfoot Training").
- Shown as the home wordmark next to the mascot logo.
- Team label (eyebrow on home): **Jr Rangers 10U**.
- "Hockey" is part of the brand name; don't use it as generic chrome elsewhere.

## Mascot / logo
- The logo is the **Bigfoot hockey mascot** (brown sasquatch, green gloves,
  stick, skates, ice spray, puck).
- It sits **free-standing and transparent — never in a box, tile, or border.**
- Files: `logo.png` (transparent master) + `logo-512/180/120/64.png` (square,
  padded — favicon / phone home-screen icon only).
- The current PNG is a screenshot crop = placeholder. Replace with the real
  **SVG** when available (crisp at any size). Never fabricate a logo.

## Personality
Fun, encouraging, a little bold — a cool teammate, not a schoolteacher. Bigfoot
is tough-but-friendly: strong, never mean. Energy and confidence, never trash
talk or pressure.

## Color identity
- Rooted in a **deep forest green** dark theme (the team's green).
- Green is the accent throughout (eyebrows, active states, leaderboard bars,
  primary buttons). Tokens live in `visual-design.md`; they flip cleanly between
  dark and light mode.

## Slogan / motto
- Footer slogan is a short player-facing rally line, **4–6 words**.
- Current placeholder: **"Compete. Improve. Have fun."**
- Use the team's real Jr. Rangers motto if the coach provides one. **Do not
  invent a motto and present it as the team's** — placeholder until confirmed.

## Content integrity (non-negotiable)
Never fabricate training content, drills, reps, videos, or a motto. Build only
from what the coach provides. When content is pending, build the real structure
(empty, honest) — never a fake or "coming soon" dead end. See `ux-writing.md`.
